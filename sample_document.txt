Company Vacation Policy

Overview
All full-time employees are entitled to paid vacation days as part of their employment benefits package.

Vacation Allowance
- Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid vacation per year
- Part-time employees receive vacation days prorated based on hours worked
- Vacation days accrue on a monthly basis (1.25 days per month)

Eligibility
- New employees must complete a 90-day probation period before using vacation days
- Vacation days begin accruing from the first day of employment
- Unused vacation days from the probation period can be used after probation ends

Usage Policy
- Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance
- Requests are subject to manager approval based on business needs
- Maximum consecutive vacation days: 10 days (unless pre-approved)
- Employees cannot take vacation during designated blackout periods

Carryover
- Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next calendar year
- Carried-over days must be used within the first quarter (March 31st)
- Days not used by the deadline will be forfeited

Special Circumstances
- Medical leave is separate from vacation time
- Bereavement leave does not count against vacation days
- Jury duty is considered paid leave, not vacation

Contact
For questions about vacation policy, contact HR at hr@company.com or extension 4567.
